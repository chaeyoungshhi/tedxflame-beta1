The Theme

WILDFIRE: What Burns, Becomes

The ninth edition of TEDxFLAME University explores the idea of a wildfire: a single spark with the power to ignite something far greater than itself. Like a flame that grows beyond its beginnings, every idea, belief and act of courage carries the potential to spread, challenge conventions and transform the world around us. A wildfire is a reminder that change rarely begins on a grand scale; sometimes, all it takes is one person daring to think differently, one voice refusing to be silenced, or one idea finding its way to the right minds.

Through this theme, we aim to inspire attendees to embrace the sparks within them, question the familiar and recognise the power of ideas that refuse to remain contained. Wildfire represents the courage to create, the conviction to challenge and the collective energy that turns individual thoughts into movements. When a spark finds its purpose, it can travel far beyond its origin, set new possibilities in motion and leave behind a world transformed.

Voices that spark

Dushyant Dubey

Social Activist

To many in Bengaluru, Dushyant Dubey is simply "St Broseph", internet slang for a brother you can always turn to. The press calls him the "Batman of Bengaluru". His Broseph Foundation brings together thousands of volunteers in four cities to help people facing abuse, harassment and cybercrime, free of charge.

Neha Panchamiya

Wildlife Conservationist

Neha Panchamiya has a master's in human nutrition and a background in graphic design. At 23 she took an unexpected turn and founded the RESQ Charitable Trust. Nearly two decades later, RESQ is one of Maharashtra's leading wildlife rescue organisations. Working with the state Forest Department, it treats everything from injured birds to trapped leopards.

About

FLAME University

FLAME University is one of India's leading institutions of liberal education, dedicated to nurturing both intellect and individuality. Set on a sprawling green campus near Pune, FLAME brings together students, scholars and practitioners who believe that learning across disciplines is the surest way to think freely, question boldly and build what comes next.

TEDxFLAME University is an independently organised, student-led event that carries that spirit onto the stage: ideas that cross boundaries, speakers who challenge the familiar and an audience ready to carry the spark forward.
